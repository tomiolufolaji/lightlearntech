This policy explains, in plain English, what personal data LightLearnTech Initiative ("LightLearnTech", "we", "us") collects through this website, why we collect it and how we look after it. We process personal data in line with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023** (NDPA) and guidance from the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

## Who we are

LightLearnTech Initiative is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us about privacy at any time at [{{ site.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}).

## What data we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us through the forms on this website:

- **Contact form:** your name, email address, enquiry type, subject and message.
- **Volunteer form:** your full name, email address, and, if you choose to give them, your phone number, location (city or state), area of interest, availability and message.
- **Partner form:** your organisation's name, the contact person's name, email address, type of partnership, message and, if you choose to give it, a phone number.

Please do not include sensitive information (such as health details) or information about children in your message.

## Why we use it

We use this information only to:

- reply to your enquiry;
- consider and arrange volunteering, including the background checks described in our [Safeguarding Statement](/safeguarding/) if you go on to work with children;
- discuss and manage partnerships and offers of support.

Our lawful basis is your consent when you submit a form, and our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us. We will not add you to a mailing list or send you marketing.

## Who sees it

Your information is seen only by the LightLearnTech team members who need it to respond to you. To run the website we use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf:

- **Netlify** hosts this website and receives form submissions, which it forwards to our email inbox.
- **Google (Gmail)** provides the email inbox where submissions are received.

These providers may store data outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we rely on their contractual and security safeguards, as the NDPA requires. We do not share your data with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

## We never sell your data

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data.

## How long we keep it

- Form submissions are forwarded to our email inbox. Copies held by our form provider are deleted at least every 30 days.
- We keep enquiry emails for no longer than two years after our last contact with you.
- If you become a volunteer or partner, we keep the information we need for as long as you work with us, and for as long afterwards as safeguarding or legal duties require.

## Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
- ask us to correct it;
- ask us to delete it;
- object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it;
- withdraw your consent at any time.

To make any of these requests, including **asking us to delete your data**, email [{{ site.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}) with the subject line "Data request". We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## Cookies and analytics

This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels or analytics tools. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the website secure and working. If we ever add a privacy-friendly analytics tool, we will update this policy first.

## Children

This website and its forms are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through this website.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
