LightLearnTech Initiative is committed to the safety and wellbeing of every child it works with.

## People who work with children

Everyone working with children on behalf of LightLearnTech, including staff and volunteers, is background-checked and follows our code of conduct.

## Photographs and personal details

Photos of children are published only with recorded consent from a parent or guardian. We never publish children's names, exact locations or any other details that could identify them.

## Reporting a concern

If you have a concern about the safety or wellbeing of a child connected with our work, please report it confidentially to [{{ site.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}). Every concern is taken seriously.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services on **112**.
