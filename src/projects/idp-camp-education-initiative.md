purpose: To strengthen foundational learning for displaced children and give teachers the resources they need.
findings: >-
  Children were attending classes, but many had significant gaps in foundational
  Mathematics and English. Some struggled to understand complete English instructions,
  and class levels did not always reflect actual learning abilities. Teachers had very
  limited resources, with no clear curriculum, structured lesson sequence or adequate
  learning materials.

  - Mathematics, English and Science textbooks and curriculum materials

  - A limited number of laptops for lesson preparation and digital learning
  - As students progress, programming resources and robotics kits
